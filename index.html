How it works

  • The model learns from thousands of past matches, ball by ball.
  • For each new match it weighs recent form, player ratings, the ground and, once known, the toss and the playing XIs.
  • A prediction is posted about 45 minutes before the start and updated once after the toss. The last one before the first ball is the one that counts in the record.
  • A percentage is a chance, not a promise. A 60% pick should still lose about 4 times in 10.
  • The model is machine learning, a kind of AI: it finds patterns in past matches. Nobody adjusts its numbers by hand.
