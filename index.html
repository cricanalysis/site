How it works

  • This is AI and statistical analysis, built on deep research into thousands of past matches, ball by ball.
  • For each new match it weighs many inputs, among them recent form, player ratings, the ground and, once known, the toss and the playing XIs.
  • An early call is posted about a day ahead, from the data alone. It is updated about 45 minutes before the start and again after the toss. The last call before the first ball is the only one that counts in the record.
  • A percentage is a chance, not a promise. A 60% pick should still lose about 4 times in 10.
  • The numbers come from machine learning and statistics, not from opinion. Nobody adjusts them by hand.
  • The calls are made by an automatic system. A power cut or a lost internet connection can make a call late, or leave a match without one. A missed match is left out of the record, and nothing is ever filled in afterwards.
  • AI and statistical analysis can make mistakes, and it will. That is why every call is graded in public.
